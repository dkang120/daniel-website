The idea

People hate being sold to but love to buy, so I ask more than I pitch.

How I run a call
  1. I · Open (10%)Who I am, why I’m calling, why now. No fake rapport.
  2. II · Discover (80%)They talk. Where they are, what success looks like, what happens if nothing changes.
  3. III · Close (10%)Tie the offer to the pains they named, get a small yes, state the price, then stay quiet.
  4. IV · Follow upCheck in after a yes. After a “not yet,” recap in their words and follow up when agreed.
