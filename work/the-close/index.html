No. 03 — Sales + Process

The Close

How I sell. One framework, used across university recruitment, high-ticket coaching and gym memberships.

Roles
Recruitment associate · Sales closer · Lead service specialist
Timeline
Jan 2026 – present
Where
Santa Clarita · remote · Dublin, CA
Tools
Phone · video calls · facility tours

01

The challenge

Three very different sales motions — cold calls to prospective students, high-ticket coaching calls, and walk-in gym tours — with the same job: turn interest into a decision.

02

The framework

  1. I · Discovery

    Open by surfacing the prospect's pain points and goals.

  2. II · The offer

    Present the offer against the needs they just described.

  3. III · Benefit stack

    Stack the benefits so the value is clear.

  4. IV · Urgency

    Use loss aversion and scarcity to move toward a decision.

  5. V · Same-call decision

    Ask for the commitment on the call.
