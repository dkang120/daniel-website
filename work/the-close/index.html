The Close

No. 03 — Sales + Process

How I sell. One framework, used across university recruitment, high-ticket coaching and gym memberships.

Roles
Recruitment associate · Sales closer · Lead service specialist
Timeline
Jan 2026 – present
Where
Santa Clarita · remote · Dublin, CA
Tools
Phone · video calls · facility tours
The challenge

Three very different sales motions — cold calls to prospective students, high-ticket coaching calls, and walk-in gym tours — with the same job: turn interest into a decision.

Approach
  1. I · DiscoveryOpen by surfacing the prospect's pain points and goals.
  2. II · The offerPresent the offer against the needs they just described.
  3. III · Benefit stackStack the benefits so the value is clear.
  4. IV · UrgencyUse loss aversion and scarcity to move toward a decision.
  5. V · Same-call decisionAsk for the commitment on the call.
Results
  • 60%tour-to-member conversion at Fitness 19
  • 14Kaudience I sold into
  • 1,000+prospective students called
  • 3states in the recruitment funnel
Where I’ve run it

Cold-calling across California, Arizona and Texas using the 7 P’s; closing high-ticket coaching offers on commission; and converting leads and walk-ins at Fitness 19 while meeting or exceeding monthly targets.
