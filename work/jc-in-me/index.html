JC in Me

I ran the Instagram for JC in Me, a faith-based shop in Santa Clarita, end to end: strategy, content, design and posting. See the account ↗

Role
Social media manager (solo)
Timeline
2025 – 2026
Tools
Claude, ChatGPT, Canva, Gamma

Results · Last 90 days

  • 155K+views (+400%)
  • 1,000+followers (+51%)
  • 69%views from non-followers
01

SituationWhere it started

  • JC in Me is a faith-based gift shop and gathering place on Main Street in Newhall, CA.
  • The owner needed someone to run the shop’s Instagram end to end.
02

ObstacleWhat stood in the way

  • No consistent look, voice or posting system.
  • One-person shop: content had to be fast to make and true to the brand.
03

ActionWhat I did

  • Rebranded the feed with one template: bold headline, soft lifestyle photo, short line in the brand’s voice.
  • Built recurring series: meet the people, product stories and “this is what it looks like” posts, plus Reels.
  • Sped up production with an AI-assisted workflow in Claude, ChatGPT, Canva and Gamma.
  • Shifted to a Reels-led mix to reach people beyond the existing followers.
04

ResultWhat it meant

  • First 30 days: 3.5% interaction rate on 18,500+ views.
  • Last 90 days: 155,000+ views (+400%) and 1,000+ followers, with 69% of views from non-followers and 1,000+ interactions and 450+ profile visits a month.

“He understood our brand voice and target audience rapidly, and every piece of content he created felt completely true to who we are.” Leslie Bess, owner
