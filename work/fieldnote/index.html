Fieldnote

A brand concept for a fresh-filled cologne for college students. Each bottle is filled the day it’s ordered and labeled with the buyer’s name, city and date. I named and positioned the brand, built the deck and designed the storefront below.

  1. ChallengeFind out if college students want a cologne made for them, before making anything.
  2. ApproachNamed and positioned the brand: fresh-filled on order, labeled with the buyer’s name, city and date. Built an editorial identity, the deck and a live storefront.
  3. OutcomeA complete concept, ready for a small sample-pack demand test before any production.
