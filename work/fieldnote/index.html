No. 02 — Brand + Design

Fieldnote

A fresh-filled cologne brand for college students. I took it from a name to a business plan to a live storefront at fieldnotela.com.

Role
Founder — brand, strategy, design
Timeline
Oct 2026 – present
Status
Pre-launch · validating demand
Tools
Figma · live site at fieldnotela.com

01

The challenge

Fragrance is usually sold as a finished bottle off a shelf. Fieldnote's idea: fill each bottle the day it's ordered and label it with the buyer's name, city and fill date — at a price that works for students.

02

What I did

  1. 01

    Name and position it

    Chose the name Fieldnote and built the brand around one promise: made the day you order it.

  2. 02

    Plan the business

    A full plan covering market sizing, competitive analysis, marketing, operations and a three-year financial model.

  3. 03

    Design the storefront in Figma

    A warm editorial system — espresso, bone, linen and a clay accent — with product pages, a discovery set and a photo guide.

  4. 04

    Ship it, then test demand

    A live site with six house scents and a label builder. Next: 2ml sample packs to measure real demand before making house scents.

04

Why it matters

Validate before you scale: the site and brand are real, but the next step is proving people want it — with sample packs, before any inventory.
