ModelLab

No. 04 — Founder Project

Founded and built an investing education site for beginners: every investing philosophy as an example portfolio to explore and compare, plus an AI text buddy that keeps you on track with your model.

Role
Founder
Timeline
Nov 2026 – present
Where
modellab.hidanielkang.com
Built with
Figma · Claude API · Twilio · Stripe · Klaviyo · Cloudflare Workers
The challenge

Investing content mostly explains philosophies in the abstract. I wanted beginners to see each one as an actual portfolio they can open, browse and compare, and then get real help sticking with it, in a product that feels like a startup, not a blog.

Approach
  1. Concept and brandDefined the purpose, what ModelLab offers and how it benefits beginners, and chose the name.
  2. Rule-based portfoliosEvery portfolio follows published rules or an industry-standard method, never hand-picked, so there is no bias.
  3. Product and designA Library, a match quiz, Compare and interactive tools, designed in Figma first around startup sites like Turbo, then recoded in HTML with a Gen Z, social-first look.
  4. The text buddyMade an opt-in SMS buddy the core of the product: an AI coach (Claude, on Twilio) that knows your model, replies instantly and checks in when the market gets wild, without ever giving stock advice.
  5. Launch and monetizationLaunched on my own subdomain with a Lab Drop that adds one model at a time, starting with Dogs of the Dow, and a $4.99/mo Pro plan on Stripe for more buddy replies, with a free tier to start.
What I learned
  1. Find the one thing people come back forThe site felt too busy, so I made the text buddy the core and turned everything else into a path toward it.
  2. Free first, then chargeI dropped the password and email gate to build an audience, then added a paid plan only once the buddy gave people something worth paying for.
  3. Take my own judgment outRemoving hand-picking from portfolio selection made the product more credible.
  4. Pick a cadence I can keepOne new model a week is a content system I can sustain, and it gives the list a reason to open emails.
  5. Design before codeWorking out decisions in Figma first made them faster and cheaper to change.
Why it matters

ModelLab is a hands-on product exercise in positioning, a funnel, a content cadence and a freemium subscription, the same skills I bring to any consumer or community brand. For education, not investment advice.
