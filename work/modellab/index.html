ModelLab

I built ModelLab as an independent beginner-investing product: a plain-language model library, guided tools and an AI text buddy for people building their first $1,000. Visit ModelLab ↗

Role
Founder · product, growth and lifecycle
Timeline
Nov 2026 – present
Status
Live product · acquisition test in progress

What is live now

  • 50+SEO landing pages
  • 13week email series
  • Freeplan + optional $4.99/mo Pro
01

ProblemWhat I saw

  • Beginner investors meet jargon, disconnected tools and advice that assumes too much.
  • Most products lead with market data; ModelLab needed to lead with behavior and a clear next step.
02

ProductWhat I built

  • A library that turns investing philosophies into comparable example portfolios.
  • A match quiz, “Now what?” flow and simulators that move people from learning to a personal plan.
  • A text buddy that remembers the user’s goal and frequency while avoiding trades, stock picks and account access.
03

FunnelHow acquisition works

  • Search and paid-social visitors enter through a model, quiz or “Now what?” question.
  • Every path demonstrates value before asking for a phone number.
  • PostHog measures the onboarding steps; Klaviyo runs consent, SMS and the 13-week lifecycle series.
04

Current resultWhat is verified

The product, free plan and optional Pro plan are live. The first Meta Ads acquisition test is in progress, so I’m measuring click-to-quiz, quiz completion, text-buddy starts and consented signups before claiming or scaling a result.
