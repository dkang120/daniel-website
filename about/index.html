About / 01

I notice what makes people show up.

I’m drawn to the place where content, design and technology meet, and to how combining them changes the way people live, create and connect. Nothing moves me more than watching someone grow in something they love.

That’s why I’m a marketer who knows how to close.

I’ve worked both ends of the funnel, building audiences and communities online, then turning that interest into sign-ups in person.
